Lights Out · Privacy Policy

Support

Something not working, or a question about Lights Out? Email mhalrebh@hotmail.com (or tap Contact Support in the app's Options). I read every message and usually reply within a few days. If you're reporting a bug, include your iPhone model and iOS version.

I bought a pass but my hearts aren't unlimited

Passes start right after the purchase goes through. If it didn't start, fully close the app and open it again. If it's still not active, email me with the time you bought it.

My progress disappeared

Your levels, stars, coins and items are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app, or setting up a phone without restoring from a backup, removes them. I can't recover them from my side, but write to me and I'll look into it.

The game is too scary

Turn on Gentler Scares in Options (the gear on the home screen). A lost run then ends without the face.

No sound, or too much vibration

Sound and Vibration each have their own switch in Options. The game also follows your phone's silent switch.

My invite code or reward didn't work

Invites need you to be signed in to iCloud on your phone. Check that you typed your friend's code correctly, and that you haven't already used a code. If it still fails, email me the code.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it, Options has a Privacy Choices button to change your ad choices. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
