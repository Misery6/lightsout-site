Privacy Policy

Lights Out · Last updated November 9, 2026

Lights Out is a game made by Mohammad Al Rebh ("I", "me"). This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there are no accounts, your game progress stays on your phone, and I never see your name, email or payment details. Some data does leave your device for ads, anonymous usage stats, leaderboards and invites. Each of these is described below.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, hearts, items, the outfits you pick, your Daily results and streak, and your settings (Sound, Vibration, Gentler Scares) are stored locally on your iPhone. I can't see them, and I can't recover them if you delete the app.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: video ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional ads between levels. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • A device identifier, such as the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) or an identifier limited to this app
  • Advertising data, such as which ads were shown and how you interacted with them
  • Performance and crash data, such as app launch time or crash logs

This data goes to Google and its advertising partners, not to me. Google's own policies apply to it: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking. The app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, ads still appear, but they aren't personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe and the UK. Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load.
  • US states. If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for ads. Ads will still appear, but they won't be personalized.
  • Changing your mind. If either of the last two applies to you, a Privacy Choices button appears in the app's Options so you can change your choice at any time.

Anonymous usage stats

The app uses TelemetryDeck to count a few anonymous events, such as how often the share and leaderboard screens are opened. TelemetryDeck doesn't collect names, emails, IP addresses or advertising identifiers, and the stats can't be linked back to you. It's used only to see which features people use. TelemetryDeck's policy: telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your level progress, Daily and endless scores are sent to Apple's leaderboards and achievements, where other players can see them next to your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Invites

The invite rewards use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). When you open your invite code or use a friend's, the app saves a small record with your invite code, the code you used, and an anonymous, app-specific iCloud ID, so both players can get their rewards. It doesn't include your name, email or Apple Account details. Nothing is saved unless you use the invite feature.

Purchases

The unlimited-hearts passes are bought through Apple. I never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The app only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase went through.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send an occasional announcement, such as when new levels are added. Notifications are delivered by Apple, and the app doesn't use them to collect any data. You can turn them off any time in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Sharing

When you share a Daily result, an invite or a score, the app prepares the text or image and iOS's share sheet sends it wherever you choose. I don't receive a copy.

What I don't do

  • I don't create accounts or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • I don't run my own servers that store your data.
  • I don't sell your data.

Children

Lights Out is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because I don't hold personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for me to access or delete. Ad data held by Google is governed by Google's tools and policies, and you can use the choices above. If you'd like to ask me anything about this policy, or make a request under the privacy law where you live, write to me and I'll reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
